Privacy Policy

CESDA — Center for Evidence Synthesis, Support & Development in Africa — respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect when you use cesdaf.org, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you submit a consultation request or contact form, we collect the information you provide: name, email, organisation, and the message itself. We may also log basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited for security and analytics.

How we use it

We use the information you provide only to respond to your enquiry, deliver the requested services, and improve our website. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

Cookies

Our site may use minimal, first-party cookies for session management and analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings without affecting access to the site.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to info@cesdaf.org.
