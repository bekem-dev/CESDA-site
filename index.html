Who we are

CESDA is a pioneering private limited company founded by EIDM champions with 20+ years of collective experience in research, policy, and development. Established as an independent initiative, CESDA works to transform how evidence informs policy and programming across Ethiopia and Africa.

Vision

To be Africa's premier hub to advance evidence-informed policy and development.

Mission

To drive evidence-informed decision-making in Africa by ensuring access to high-quality policy-relevant evidence, empowering policymakers and programmers, and fostering collaboration for equitable and sustainable development.

Core values

Our work is anchored in integrity, professionalism, and ethical practice. We support the achievement of high-impact policy outcomes through a dedication to excellence, utilising participatory and collaborative frameworks that bring diverse voices to the table.
